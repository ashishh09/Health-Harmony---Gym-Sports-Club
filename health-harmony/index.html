About Health & Harmony

MORE THAN JUST A GYM

हेल्थ ॲंड हार्मनी – फिटनेस, क्रीडा आणि संपूर्ण आरोग्याचे केंद्र

Health & Harmony is Chhatrapati Sambhajinagar’s premier fitness and athletic sports club. Located conveniently opposite Kittos Appe in Town Center, CIDCO, we provide an expansive multi-discipline wellness destination designed for individuals and families who demand excellence in their lifestyle.

From our multi-station cardio and strength training floors to our climate-controlled indoor swimming pool and international-standard badminton and squash courts, we offer a comprehensive active sanctuary under one roof.

WHY CHOOSE HEALTH & HARMONY

Combining world-class sports amenities, disciplined coaching, and a welcoming community right in the heart of Chhatrapati Sambhajinagar.

Multiple Facilities Under One Roof

No need for separate memberships. Access gym, indoor pool, badminton, squash, and outdoor turf in a single integrated lifestyle complex.

Diverse Gym Equipment

Equipped with Cybex Arc trainers, cable cross stations, heavy dumbbell racks, power cages, and specialized functional movement tools.

Indoor Swimming Experience

Protected from harsh weather, rain, and UV rays. Clean, filtered water with professional lifeguards and dedicated coaching slots.

Sports & Recreational Activities

From competitive badminton singles to high-tempo squash and outdoor CrossFit workouts, stay engaged with variety every single day.

Supportive Training Environment

A positive, motivating atmosphere suitable for both beginners embarking on their health journey and experienced athletes aiming for new PRs.

Convenient Town Center Location

Centrally situated opposite Kittos Appe in Town Center CIDCO. Ample parking, wide approach roads, and open daily until 9:30 PM.
